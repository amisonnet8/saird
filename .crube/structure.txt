crates/	The Rust crates of the workspace, one per layer of the trusted base
	saird-core/	The small audited layer: parsing, types and constraints, the expression evaluator, kv ranges and inclusion, the checker, seal hashes, and the formats and verifier of receipts
		Cargo.toml	Package of saird-core; no dependency on the other crates, and as few dependencies as possible
		src/	Source of saird-core
			lib.rs	The library root of saird-core; a placeholder until its first module
			syntax/	Reading contracts into a syntax tree (docs/reference/syntax.md); only the grammar is checked here
				mod.rs	The entry of syntax: parse_contract, the syntax tree, positions and errors
				ast.rs	The syntax tree of contracts, with positions
				error.rs	Positions in a source, and the syntax error with line and column
				lexer.rs	Indentation, continued lines, blocks and tokens
				parser.rs	The parser of contracts and expressions, with its tests (including the ReserveStock example of the docs)
			types/	Types of contract values and the WIT skeleton of a box
				mod.rs	Ty (the type of a value or expression), TypeError, and Scope, which types expressions
				wit.rs	contract_wit: the WIT package, world and call function of a contract
				timestamp.rs	Timestamp: seconds and nanoseconds since the epoch in UTC, read and written as RFC 3339 with Z
	saird-runtime/	The widely used layer: the host, the standard boxes kv and clock, the test runner, and issuing receipts
		Cargo.toml	Package of saird-runtime; depends on saird-core only
		src/	Source of saird-runtime
			lib.rs	The library root of saird-runtime; a placeholder until its first module
	saird/	The command saird and what it shows to people (permission summary, coverage table)
		Cargo.toml	Package of the command saird; depends on saird-core and saird-runtime
		src/	Source of the command saird
			lib.rs	The library root of saird (display); a placeholder until the command is added
